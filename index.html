About Wilies Hospital

Care that feels personal.

Wilies Hospital is a private hospital in Maseru, Lesotho, serving patients from the surrounding community with a range of healthcare services. Our website is designed to make it easier to find the information you need, connect with the hospital and know what to expect.

Whether you need a consultation, maternity-related care, diagnostics, emergency assistance or general hospital information, our team is ready to help guide you to the right point of care.
