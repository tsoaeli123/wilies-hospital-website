About Wilies Hospital

Healthcare rooted in care, trust and community.

Wilies Hospital is a private institution located in Khubetsoana, Bochabela II, Maseru, Lesotho, established in 2003. We provide comprehensive healthcare delivery services to local, governmental, non-governmental, and international people.

Our focus is to promote, protect, and restore employee health to minimize sick leave and maximize company output. We have won the 1st Prize many times in succession since 2008 among hospitals/clinics by PMR Africa (SADC assessment) in Lesotho for excellence in health delivery. Our latest award was on 11/08/2022 (Diamond).
